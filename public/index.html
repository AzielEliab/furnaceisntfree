The headline and the invoice

Headline: “Buy a new AC and get a furnace free.” A promotion is a claim. These are the questions that sit underneath it.

The promise

What does “free” actually include? Which furnace model, and what allowance is written down?

The specification

Some offers may be built around a basic 80% AFUE, single-stage furnace. That is a configuration to check, not a fact about every promotion. Ask for model, efficiency, staging, capacity, venting, and compatibility.

Evidence label: analysis / inference from this site’s build notes, not an industry census.

The real total

Equipment plus labor plus required work, minus only the credits the contract actually applies. A credit against equipment is not the same as a free installed system.

Four questions before you accept

1

What exact furnace model is included?

2

What is the equipment-only allowance or credit?

3

What labor, materials, accessories, and modifications are additional?

4

What is the complete installed price, and the comparable price without the promotion?

A balanced starting point

Sometimes replacement is sensible. Sometimes repair is reasonable. The answer depends on diagnosis, condition, safety findings from a qualified person, compatibility, efficiency, parts availability, expected remaining life, and what you want. Age or surface rust, by itself, does not prove a furnace is unsafe. Get findings in writing.

This site does not name companies. It names the parts of an offer: equipment, allowance, labor, modifications, exclusions, and total. It does not turn one quote into a claim about the trade.
