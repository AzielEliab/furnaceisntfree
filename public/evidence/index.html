Method

Evidence and method

The Business Ambiguity Lattice on this site is a practical way to unpack a commercial claim. It is this site’s operational method. A dedicated lattice paper was not the source, and the method is not presented as a verdict.

The Custodial Knowledge Model (Aziel Eliab, August 2026) is a conceptual working paper cited in the build notes for stewardship and correction. It is not an independent validation of any HVAC price.

01

Claim

What is promised? Example: “Get a furnace free.”

02

Reference

Compared with what? MSRP, list price, allowance, or a prior selling price.

03

Conditions

What qualifies it? A basic model, excluded labor, required add-ons.

04

Total

Equipment + labor + modifications + fees − credits the contract applies.

05

Verification

Itemized quote, model numbers, terms, invoices, a comparable offer.
