What matters most to you

Should I replace my HVAC?

This site cannot answer that for a particular house. A sensible choice depends on a written diagnosis, measurements, safety findings from a qualified person, compatibility, efficiency, parts, remaining life, and your priorities. Use the questions above, and get the answers in writing.
