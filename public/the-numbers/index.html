Definitions and calculator

The numbers

Separate the kinds of price before you subtract anything. The calculator uses only the figures you type.

Price words that are not interchangeable

TermWhat it is
MSRPA manufacturer list price. Not proof of what buyers paid.
Advertised reference priceA price a promotion compares itself with. Ask when it applied and whether the product was offered at that price.
Actual transaction priceWhat a buyer paid in a real sale. A single quote does not establish this for a market.
AllowanceA credit the offer says it will apply. Read where it can be applied.
Installed costEquipment, labor, materials, modifications, and fees, minus only the credits the contract applies.

What does “free” actually mean?

Enter actual written figures. Leave unknown fields blank.

  • Net equipment after allowance = max(0, equipment price − allowance).
  • If the allowance is larger than the equipment price, the extra credit is shown on its own line and is not subtracted from labor.
  • Installation and additional = labor + materials/modifications + other required charges. If any of those three is blank, the sum stays unknown.
  • Incremental installed furnace cost = net equipment after allowance + installation and additional.
  • Labor-to-equipment ratio = labor ÷ equipment-only price, only when the equipment price is greater than zero.
  • Package versus alternative = package total − AC-only comparison price. It is never built by adding the other lines.
